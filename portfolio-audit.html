Portfolio & Resume Audit: Ashok Barua

Sources: ashokbaruaakas.com (/, /resume, /open-source, sitemap, robots, 404), the resume printed to PDF with the site's own "Download PDF" button (it calls window.print()), the GitHub API, Packagist, and the linked product sites. Checked 12 Oct 2026. [V] = verified, [A] = my assumption or inference.

1. Who I understand you to be

A Chattogram, Bangladesh–based (UTC+6) full-stack engineer with about 8.5 years of work history (Mar 2018 to now). Your stack is PHP/Laravel + Vue/TypeScript/Inertia. You present yourself as a "Senior Full-Stack Engineer — Fintech & Multi-Tenant SaaS". Your experience is SiPay (a Turkish banking payment platform, 2021–23, at Softrobotics) and, currently, Grow More Gaze (a multi-tenant HRM/payroll/payments SaaS where you say you own the full lifecycle). You have a small open-source footprint: one merged inertia-laravel PR, the SoftPulze org (LaraVibe starter kit and standards package), and clawkit (a Docker/OpenClaw wrapper).

Contradiction (this is a finding): the headline says "Senior", the year count says "7+", and your current title is "Full Stack Developer". That is one level below the "Senior Software Engineer" title you held before it. The story reads as a demotion unless you explain it.

2. Executive summary

The positioning is solid and specific: fintech payments plus multi-tenant SaaS is a sellable niche. The site itself is technically clean (SSR, canonical, OG, JSON-LD, sitemap, real 404). What holds back interviews is the evidence layer. There is almost no impact data, the seniority signals contradict each other, there is no LinkedIn, and the "downloadable resume" is a browser print whose text order breaks ATS parsing.

  1. No real resume file, and the print PDF scrambles the experience section for ATS. In the PDF text layer, both job headers come first and their bullets are grouped after them. Company and date are also fused together ("Grow More GazeOct 2023").
  2. Mixed seniority signals: the headline says "Senior", the current title is "Full Stack Developer", you claim "7+ years" while the dates show 8.5, and "Team Lead" is a project badge even though it lasted only 4 months.
  3. No LinkedIn anywhere. It is missing from the site, the resume, JSON-LD sameAs, and GitHub. International recruiters verify candidates there first.
  4. Almost no outcome metrics. The only number is "40,000+ customers", and it is undefined. There is no transaction volume, uptime, latency, revenue, team size or tenant count. Every bullet describes an activity, not a result.
  5. The open-source data is stale and inflated. LaraVibe-Vue shows stars: 0 but has 2. Six of the seven "contributions" are your own repos or org. The real signal (the merged inertia-laravel #700) is lost among them.
